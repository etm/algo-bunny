Algo wants to eat the carrot. But this time the carrot is on a
different island.

The flower in front of Algo is his friend and tells him where he has
to jump to. Algo has to remember what the flower says
![](commands/memorize_flower.svg).

What the flower says is then stored in his big brain (bottom right)
![](assets/brain.svg).

When Algo jumps to the spot he remembered
![](commands/jump_brain.svg), he can eat the carrot.
